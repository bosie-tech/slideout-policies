Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap an arrow on the board to slide it in the direction it points. If the arrow's path is blocked by another arrow, the tap won't move it — that's a hint about what to clear first. Clear all arrows to complete a level.

I don't see any ads, then suddenly I do — why?

SlideOut shows occasional interstitial ads to support the game. We never show ads mid-puzzle — only between levels or on a restart, and never more than once per few minutes.

How do I disable personalized ads?

Open your device settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → SlideOut and turn off "Allow Tracking". You'll still see ads, but they won't use your device identifier.

How do I reset my progress?

In the app, go to Settings → Reset Progress. This deletes all level completion data stored on your device. There is no cloud save.

I think I found a bug — what should I send?

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 14) and iOS version
  • A screenshot or short screen recording if possible
